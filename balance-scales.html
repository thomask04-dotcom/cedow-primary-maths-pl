Balance Scales
=
Mass: drag things into the pans. The heavier side goes down. Measure an object by adding cubes to the other side until the scales balance. Big things aren't always heavier! Equal sign: "=" means "is the same amount as". If both sides are the same amount, the scales balance.
Settings:
Number balance: each weight is worth the number of its peg. Tap a number (or drag a weight from the box) to hang a weight; drag a weight off to remove it. When both sides are worth the same, the balance is level and the equals sign is true. "⇄ Flip it" swaps the two sides: 2 + 5 + 1 = 5 + 3 becomes 5 + 3 = 2 + 5 + 1, and it still balances.
